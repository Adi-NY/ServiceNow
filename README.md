<h1>ServiceNow - Homelab showcasing real Helpdesk Environment</h1>


<h2>Description</h2>
A full end-to-end help desk simulation built in a ServiceNow developer instance, covering the complete ticket lifecycle from creation to resolution. Scenarios are based on real incidents encountered in IT support roles.
<br />


## How ServiceNow Was Used

## Incident Management
- Created **INC0010001** (Outlook access issue) and assigned it to the Help Desk.
- Created **INC0010008** (Accounting shared drive outage), identified a probable VLAN issue, and escalated to Network Operations.
- Resolved **INC0010001** through Office repair and documented troubleshooting steps.

## SLA Management
- Configured a **Priority 1 Resolution (1 Hour)** SLA.
- Monitored Task SLA metrics including remaining time, elapsed time, and breach status.

## Knowledge Management
- Authored and published **KB0010001**: *How to Resolve Outlook Not Opening in Windows 10*.
- Included troubleshooting steps, escalation guidance, and prevention recommendations.

## Service Requests
- Processed **REQ0000001** for an iPad request.
- Configured the device, applied MDM settings, installed approved applications, and completed delivery.

## Escalation
- Escalated **INC0010008** to Tier 2 Network Operations with documented findings and work notes.

## Reporting & Analytics
- Reviewed ServiceNow dashboards and key ITSM metrics, including incident, escalation, and change management performance indicators.
